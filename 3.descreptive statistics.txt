import pandas as pd
df = pd.read_csv("iris.csv")

# Summary statistics by species
print(df.groupby('species')['sepal_length'].describe())

# Percentiles for each species
for spec in df['species'].unique():
    print("\n", spec)
    print(df[df['species']==spec].describe())


Q1: What are measures of central tendency?
Ans: Mean, median, and mode are measures that describe the center of a dataset.

Q2: What is standard deviation?
Ans: It shows how spread out the values are from the mean. A low value means the data is close to the mean.

Q3: What is grouping in statistics?
Ans: Grouping means summarizing numerical data based on a category, like average income by age group.
